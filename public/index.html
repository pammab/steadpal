    Runway risk check

    When to use it: before raising your income from the reserve, making a big purchase, or when a client leaves.

    Methodology: 1,000 simulated futures from your own income history. How to read it: the percentage is the share of 1,000 simulated futures in which your reserve and emergency fund never run out; the shaded band is where 8 in 10 of those futures end up. Limits: it only knows your last 6 months of income and assumes the future looks like the past, so it can't see new clients, losing a client, or one-off costs. It is a planning aid, not a guarantee or financial advice.
